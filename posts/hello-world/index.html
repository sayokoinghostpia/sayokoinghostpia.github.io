For three decades, Harriet Mena Hill has been a resident of South East London. This area is known in the art world for its schools, such as the esteemed Camberwell College of Arts. But Hill and others who lived there for some time remember it as a historically working-class and, at times, impoverished area of the city. That impoverishment is what prompted the construction of one of Europe’s biggest social housing projects: the Aylesbury Estate, begun in 1963 and completed in 1977. In 2018, Hill’s work in arts education brought her to the site, and it led to a major shift in her broader artistic practice.
